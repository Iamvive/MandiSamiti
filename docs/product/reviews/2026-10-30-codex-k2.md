## Blocker K2-P0-1: repayment interest reduces party balance

Production reproduction through `LendTransactionUseCase` and the real repositories:

| Sequence | Hand-computed balance | App balance |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| ₹10,000 loan, then ₹1,200 paid as ₹1,000 principal + ₹200 interest | ₹9,000 receivable | ₹8,800 receivable |
| ₹1,000 loan, then ₹1,700 paid as ₹1,000 principal + ₹200 interest + ₹500 general jama | ₹500 party credit | ₹700 party credit |

Root cause: `getPartyBalance` term 10 in `AppDatabase.sq` sums `lendSettlementEntity.amount_paisa`. That field is principal plus interest. It must sum principal for split rows while retaining the amount-only fallback for legacy settlements. The intended expression is equivalent to:

```sql
CASE
  WHEN principal_paisa != 0 OR interest_paisa != 0 THEN principal_paisa
  ELSE amount_paisa
END
```

I did not change this money logic because K2 requires reporting mismatches to Claude rather than fixing them silently.

## Blocker K2-P0-2: day page labels all repayment money as principal

`DailyRegisterViewModel` still has the named `TODO(feat/bahi-claude)` and creates one `LOAN_PRINCIPAL_REPAID` line from `settlement.amountPaisa`. A ₹1,000 principal + ₹200 interest repayment therefore appears as ₹1,200 principal with no interest line. The total is unchanged, but the displayed split is false. K2 did not touch UI files.

The pure `BahiDayPageBuilder` correctly balances separate principal and interest entries; Claude needs to map `principalPaisa` and `interestPaisa` into those two entry kinds.
