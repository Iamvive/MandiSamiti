Close Day Concepts

Close day, three ways to count the galla

Your current screen shows 6 of 10 denominations and gives the shortfall the smallest box. In all three concepts the difference is the headline and every denomination is reachable without scrolling. They work: tap to count. Expected cash is ₹128 from your staging screen; the starting counts are examples.

A. Rokad tally (recommended)

Ten ledger lines and a built-in number pad. Type a count, press अगला, move down. The phone keyboard never opens.

B. Galla tiles

Laid out like drawer compartments. One tap adds one note. Chips add 10 or a गड्डी of 100 to the selected tile.

C. Gaddi counter

One denomination at a time, entered as bundles plus loose notes, the way a large pile is actually counted.

What changes in all three

Before any entry the screen says the cash is not counted, and both buttons stay off. When cash differs, the close button states the amount, and tapping it asks for a one-tap reason. Requiring that reason is new behaviour; today it is optional.
